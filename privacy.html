Privacy policy

Your recordings stay under your control.

TakeStack for iPhone keeps project media and private previews on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a TakeStack account or cloud.

Effective 1 September 2026

Information handled by the apps

TakeStack handles video and audio files you choose, recordings you make, guide tracks, project settings, manual cue positions, and information needed to render and export a video. TakeStack uses the camera, microphone, Photos, and Files only after you choose the related feature and grant the corresponding iPhone permission.

  1. Local media

    Source videos, recordings, project state, renders, and private previews remain on your iPhone unless you explicitly choose Photos, Files, or Share.

  2. Saving and sharing

    TakeStack does not send a finished video automatically. Your first proof export includes a small TakeStack brand mark. Photos, Files, and Share are separate user-initiated destinations.

  3. No advertising or automatic analytics

    The app does not include advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party product analytics. It does not automatically send project media, usage history, purchase history, or diagnostics to TakeStack.

  4. Your controls

    You can remove saved copies from Photos or Files, delete local projects inside TakeStack where that control is available, and remove all remaining app data by deleting TakeStack from your iPhone.

Website, purchases, and support

The TakeStack website does not use advertising cookies or behavioral tracking. TakeStack Pro purchases use Apple's StoreKit purchase sheet and App Store account; TakeStack does not receive your payment-card information.

  • If you email support, TakeStack receives the address and information you choose to include.
  • Verified StoreKit transaction state is used on device to unlock monthly, annual, or lifetime TakeStack Pro access and to restore purchases.
  • TakeStack does not send purchase history to a developer server.
  • Apple may provide aggregate App Store information or diagnostics that you choose to share through Apple.
  • TakeStack does not sell personal information.

Contact and changes

TakeStack is not directed to children under 13. If you have a privacy question or want to request access to or deletion of information you sent directly to TakeStack, email hello@cheerfulduck.com. This policy will be updated when the product's data practices materially change.
