Get help recording a two-part performance or combining two or four videos from Photos, reviewing the result privately, and choosing when to save or share it.

Contact

Email hello@cheerfulduck.com for app issues, feedback, or feature requests. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the step you reached, and the exact message shown on screen.

Do not email security tokens, personal file paths, purchase receipts, or recorded media unless support specifically asks for a safe sample and you choose to send it.

  1. Recording on iPhone

    Check the visible camera, microphone, storage, and headphone-route guidance before recording a guide or overdub.

  2. Starting from Photos

    Choose exactly two or four videos, mark the same visible or audible cue in each, and review the rendered file with sound.

  3. Saving or sharing

    Your first proof export is free and includes a small TakeStack brand mark. A later save or share request may offer TakeStack Pro. Closing that screen returns to your private preview.

  4. TakeStack Pro and Restore Purchases

    TakeStack Pro is available monthly, annually, or as a lifetime purchase. Eligible new annual subscribers may receive a seven-day free trial. Use Restore Purchases on the purchase screen if an existing entitlement is missing.

Privacy

TakeStack does not require an account and does not automatically send project media or app telemetry. StoreKit handles purchases; TakeStack does not receive payment-card information. Read the TakeStack privacy policy for data, permissions, retention, and deletion details. Read the TakeStack terms of use, which incorporate Apple's Standard EULA.
