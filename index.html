Two native apps. One local workflow.

The phone performs. The Mac produces.

TakeStack separates capture from editing so the recording screen stays calm and the production controls stay where there is room for them. Both devices must be on the same local network to pair and transfer takes.

iPhone · Free

TakeStack Capture

Receives the guide, records the assigned part, keeps saved takes recoverable, and sends them to Studio.

Mac · Paid

TakeStack Studio

Creates the session, organizes takes, preserves timing, builds the composition, mixes audio, and exports the video.

Clear boundaries

Your performance does not need a TakeStack cloud.

  • Local transferStudio and Capture communicate on your local network.
  • No TakeStack accountThe paired-device workflow does not depend on a hosted account.
  • No automatic postingTakeStack creates a file; you decide where and how to share it.
  • No magic import sync claimCapture preserves TakeStack timing evidence. Unrelated imports may need manual alignment.

Before you record

The important questions, answered plainly.

Need another detail? Ask TakeStack support.

Do I need both apps?

For TakeStack-controlled recording, yes: free iPhone Capture records the performance and paid Mac Studio runs the session and produces the result.

Does TakeStack upload my videos?

The core workflow transfers recordings over your local network and does not require TakeStack cloud storage. Files leave your devices only when you choose a sharing destination.

Can several people record remotely at once?

No. TakeStack currently supports one paired iPhone and one Mac on the same local network. It does not promise live remote collaboration or multi-phone synchronization.

Does it publish directly to social platforms?

No. Destination presets prepare a compatible video and hand it to your normal share or upload flow; creating the file is not publishing it.

Where are the App Store links?

The release is still completing Apple approval. This page will link directly to the public listings when available; support can confirm the current status without an obsolete web checkout.
