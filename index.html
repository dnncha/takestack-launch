One musical workflow

From separate takes to one finished video.

TakeStack focuses on the moments that matter to a musician: a guide, a shared cue, listening back, and a deliberate final save.

  1. 01

    Record two parts

    Record a guide, listen privately with headphones, and perform another part on the same iPhone.

  2. 02

    Or choose 2 or 4 takes

    Start with existing portrait videos from Photos for a duet, harmony stack, cover, or one-person band.

  3. 03

    Mark the shared cue

    Choose the same visible or audible moment in each take so the performance starts together.

  4. 04

    Review with sound

    Watch and listen to the rendered performance before deciding where the finished file goes.

  5. 05

    Save or share deliberately

    Save a copy to Photos or Files, or open the iPhone Share sheet. Nothing is posted automatically.

Made around performance

More than arranging boxes.

Generic collage tools begin with a layout. TakeStack begins with the music: keep time with a guide, add the next part, line up a cue, hear the result, then export the performance you intended.

Source videos and finished files stay under your control. TakeStack does not require an account and is not a social network.

Standalone and local-first

Your performance stays on your iPhone unless you choose to save or share it.

TakeStack uses camera, microphone, and Photos access only for the recording and media actions you choose. Learn more in the privacy policy.
