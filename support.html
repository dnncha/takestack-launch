TakeStack support

Tell us where the performance got stuck.

Get help with TakeStack Capture on iPhone or TakeStack Studio on Mac. The iPhone app can record a two-part performance or combine two or four videos from Photos without a Mac.

Contact

Email hello@cheerfulduck.com for app issues, feedback, or feature requests. Include whether you are using iPhone or Mac, the app version, the step you reached, and the exact message shown on screen.

Do not email pairing codes, security tokens, private network addresses, personal file paths, or recorded media unless support specifically asks for a safe sample and you choose to send it.

  1. Recording on iPhone

    Check the visible camera, microphone, storage, and headphone-route guidance before recording a guide or overdub.

  2. Starting from Photos

    Choose exactly two or four videos, mark the same visible or audible cue in each, and review the rendered file with sound.

  3. Saving or sharing

    Save to Photos, save to Files, and Share are separate actions. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination.

  4. Optional Studio pairing

    Keep the Mac and iPhone on the same local network, use a fresh code from Studio, and follow the recovery message if the connection changes.

Privacy

TakeStack does not require an account and does not automatically send project media or app telemetry. Read the TakeStack privacy policy for data, permissions, local transfer, retention, and deletion details.
