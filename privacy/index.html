Privacy policy

Your recordings stay under your control.

TakeStack is a local-first iPhone app. It does not require an account and does not automatically upload your source videos or finished performances.

Effective 1 September 2026

Recordings and media

TakeStack handles the videos and audio you record in the app, videos you choose from Photos, cue positions, layout choices, and rendered performance files. This information is used on your iPhone to make the video you request.

  • Camera and microphone access are used when you choose to record.
  • Photos access is used when you choose source videos or save a finished video.
  • Files and the system Share sheet are used only after you choose a save or share action and destination.
  • TakeStack does not automatically post or send your media.

Storage and deletion

Working media and settings are stored locally by TakeStack. Copies you save to Photos or Files remain there until you delete them using those apps. You can remove TakeStack’s local app data by deleting the app from your iPhone. Before deleting the app or beginning a new performance, confirm that any result you want to keep has been saved.

Analytics and tracking

TakeStack does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party product analytics. It does not automatically send project media, usage history, or diagnostics to TakeStack.

Apple may provide aggregate App Store information or diagnostics you choose to share under your Apple privacy settings.

Apple purchases

If a purchase is offered through the app, Apple processes the payment under Apple’s privacy policy. TakeStack does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide transaction information needed to recognise the purchase, provide support, process refunds, keep accounting records, and meet legal obligations.

Support messages

If you email support, TakeStack receives your email address and the information you choose to include. Support messages are used to respond to you, diagnose the issue, and maintain necessary business records. Do not send recordings or sensitive information unless support asks for a specific sample and you choose to provide it.

Contact and changes

TakeStack does not sell personal information. The app is not directed to children under 13. For a privacy question or a request concerning information you sent directly to TakeStack, email hello@cheerfulduck.com. This policy will be updated when the product’s data practices materially change.
