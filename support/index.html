TakeStack support

Keep the performance moving.

Get help with recording or importing parts, marking a cue, reviewing your video with sound, and saving or sharing the finished performance.

Contact TakeStack

Email hello@cheerfulduck.com. The subject will be “TakeStack support”. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, TakeStack version, the step you reached, and the exact message shown. Please do not attach private recordings unless support asks for a specific sample and you choose to send it.

Recording a guide and another part

Camera or microphone access

Open iPhone Settings, find TakeStack, and allow Camera and Microphone access. Return to TakeStack and try again.

Hearing the guide privately

Connect headphones before recording the next part. Confirm the guide is audible through the headphones and not the iPhone speaker.

Low storage

Video recording and rendering need free space. Check Settings → General → iPhone Storage, then free space before beginning another render.

Starting from Photos

Choose exactly two or four videos. If a video is missing, allow TakeStack access to that item or to your photo library in iPhone Settings.

Marking a shared cue

In each take, find the same clap, count-in, first note, or visible movement and mark it. A clear cue makes manual alignment more reliable.

Unexpected crop or layout

TakeStack produces a fixed vertical result. Portrait source videos fit this layout best; landscape clips may be cropped to fill their part of the frame.

Playback, saving, and sharing

No sound during review

Raise the media volume, disconnect unintended audio devices, and replay the rendered video. Check that each source video contains audible sound.

Save to Photos

Choose Save to Photos and grant add access if prompted. The finished video appears in the Photos library after the save completes.

Save to Files or share

Choose Save to Files to select a folder, or Share to open the iPhone Share sheet. TakeStack does not post or send the performance until you choose a destination.

Begin another performance

Confirm the finished video is present in your chosen destination before starting again. A saved copy is separate from the working media in TakeStack.
